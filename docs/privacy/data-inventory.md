# Data inventory: Rascend Website

Last reviewed: 31 October 2026

The website collects **no** personal data. There are no forms, accounts, cookies, analytics or advertising. Visitors' browsers request static files from the site's own domain only.

| # | Data item | Personal? | Why | Stored where | Shared with | Retention | Deletion | Protection |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Server access logs (IP address, pages requested) | Yes (IP address) | Hosting security and reliability | Netlify | Netlify (hosting provider) | Per Netlify's policy | By the hosting provider | HTTPS; provider controls |
| 2 | Emails sent to the support address | Yes | Replying to support requests | Studio mailbox | Nobody | Until no longer needed | On request by email | Mailbox account security |

## Third parties and external domains
| Domain or service | Purpose | Data sent | In privacy policy? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Netlify | Hosting and delivery | Visitor IP address and requested pages (server logs) | Yes |

The pages link to app stores and client websites. Those are ordinary links the visitor chooses to follow; they are not loaded automatically.
